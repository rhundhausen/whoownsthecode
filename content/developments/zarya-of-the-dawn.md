In February 2023 the U.S. Copyright Office decided the registration for Kris Kashtanova's comic book *Zarya of the Dawn*, made with Midjourney images. The Office allowed registration of the human-written text and the human selection and arrangement of the images, but excluded the individual AI-generated images from protection. <!-- TODO: link -->

This is the template for mixed human and AI works. Protection attaches to the human-authored layers, not the AI output. For software, the parallel is a mixed codebase: human-written portions and human selection and arrangement may be protectable, while purely generated functions are not.
