---

In [*Thaler v. Perlmutter*](https://media.cadc.uscourts.gov/opinions/docs/2025/03/23-5233.pdf), the D.C. Circuit held that works created entirely by AI are not eligible for copyright and that only humans can hold copyright protection. Thaler had tried to register AI-generated art with the machine listed as the author and was denied.

The principle applies directly to software. AI-generated code without human creative input has no author the law will recognize, so there is no copyright in it for anyone to own.
