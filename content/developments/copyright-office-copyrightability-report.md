summary: "The Office's report on the copyrightability of AI outputs says AI-assisted works may qualify, but only the human-authored portions are protected."
---

The U.S. Copyright Office published its [report on the copyrightability of AI outputs](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/), following the registration guidance it issued in 2023. Both reaffirm that copyright protection requires human authorship.

The report's position is that AI-assisted works may qualify for protection, but only the portions with meaningful human creative input are eligible. The AI-generated remainder is not. For a mixed codebase, that is the line between what you can register and what you cannot.
