title: "First decision to reject fair use for AI training"
date: 2025-02-11
lastmod: 2026-09-12
summary: "The District of Delaware rejected Ross Intelligence's fair use defense and found infringement in Thomson Reuters v. Ross."
---

In [*Thomson Reuters v. Ross*](https://www.ded.uscourts.gov/sites/ded/files/opinions/20-613_5.pdf), the legal publisher sued Ross Intelligence for allegedly scraping its copyrighted legal content to train an AI legal research tool. Ross argued its use was intermediate and transformative rather than a republishing of Westlaw materials.

In February 2025 the district court rejected that defense and found infringement. It was the first decision to deny fair use for AI training, narrowing a shield many teams assumed they had. The case is now on appeal to the Third Circuit.
